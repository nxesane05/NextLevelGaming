Our Story

Next Level Gaming Arena was established in 2023 by Johannesburg based gamer Jason Naidoo.After noticing that most local gamaers lacked access to high end equipment and structured competitive gaming opportunities, he decided to create a dediated space for them.

Jason set out to build a modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages could enjoy the latest technology, compete in esports tournaments and socialise with friends.

Today the arena hosts gaming events, esports tournaments, birthday parties, school outings and corporate team-building events, all under one roof.
